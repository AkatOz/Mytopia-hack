# island/: the face of MYTOPIA

The island draws the gateway's decision log. It never decides anything. Every animation is one row of `GET /events`, and the only things it sends back are clicks: the operator buttons, START (build 4), the six tool chips, PROMOTE and DEMOTE, the NEW AGENT form and RETIRE, each a POST that changes nothing on screen until the gateway's own event comes back (for START, the launcher's `run_started`; START's 202 answer only puts the launcher's pid in the ticker). Two exceptions, by design: when the gateway refuses a click, its sentence shows as a toast near the top (4 to 9 s, by its length) and in the ticker, for example a grant above a badge's tier (HTTP 403) or a START it cannot run (HTTP 409: `START refused: the AI brain needs a Bedrock key (scripts/bedrock_key.py); start it scripted instead`); and when a click changes nothing (a chip already in that state, or, on a gateway with build 5, a repeated REVOKE, RESTORE or KILL), the gateway's note shows in the ticker. Either way nothing was written, so there is no row. An event banner longer than 40 characters wraps and stays 4 s instead of 2.

## Answers to the brief's three questions

1. **Stack.** Plain ES modules plus **three.js 0.184** (WebGL), **no build step**. The renderer is Mytopia's `<three-d-stage>` web component: an orthographic camera, rendered at half resolution, with an ink-outline post-process for the pixel look. Every word on the island is crisp DOM laid over the canvas; inside the rooms the text is painted on 2048-pixel canvas textures, and the stage renders at full resolution while a room is open (`setPixel`, the one method added to the component this weekend). The gateway serves the `island/` folder as is; three.js and the fonts are vendored, so there are no CDN or network calls.
2. **Buildings.** An archipelago (`docs/ISLAND_V2.md`): each tool lives on its own islet, reached over one bridge with a gate and a guard. **Files** = the FILES tower, **GitHub** = the glass studio, **Mail** = the cottage, **Admin** = the courthouse, and the **gatehouse** on the main island is the gateway itself (its interior is the ledger room). Since build 3 (`docs/BUILD3.md`) there is also the **Factory island** to the south, over a plank bridge with no gate (it is the operator's own ground): the brick hall with its sawtooth roof and two chimneys (the office and the production floor are inside), the yard where built robots wait, the open inspection bay with its lift, scanner and screen, and the cart.
3. **Pre-existing vs new.** Pre-existing (from Mytopia, before this weekend): `three-d-stage.js` (one method added on Saturday night, `setPixel`) and the recipes gathered into `src/kit.js` (palette, materials, primitives), the terrain and scenery recipes, the building shapes and the small robot. Everything else is new this weekend. The full list is in [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md).

## Run it

Served by the gateway at `http://127.0.0.1:8787/` (it serves its own `/config.js`). Replays: `?replay=/replay/demo3.json` (the Factory run on real AWS, Saturday 7:55 PM: 56 rows, real hashes, so the ledger reads chain verified), `?replay=/replay/demo3-local.json` (the same flow in local mode), `?replay=/replay/demo.json` (the recorded 4:18 PM build-2 run), `?replay=fixture.json` (the contract's 21 events), `?replay=tests/additions.json` (hand-written: a per-tool revoke and a quarantine), `?replay=tests/factory.json` (hand-written: the Factory story, with placeholder hashes). Tests: `node --test island/tests/*.test.mjs` (see the suite for today's count).

**Keys.** **F** (or the FACTORY button, or a click on the Factory hall) opens the Factory office sheet: the roster and the NEW AGENT form. **G** (or a click on the gatehouse) opens the ledger room. **H** hides every panel; the pill bottom-left, or H again, brings them back. **?** opens the help sheet: six illustrated lines (the robot, a gate, the guard, the pen, the Factory, the ledger), the keys and the mouse. **T** shows every event as a table. **A** opens the architecture sheet. **R** switches between a recording and live (build 4; it used to be a one-way door into replay): on the live island it plays `fixture.json`, and in a recording it drops the `replay` parameter (any other is kept) and reloads live; a click on the grey REPLAY badge does the same. **C** (build 4) puts the camera back to the opening view: target, distance, angle and zoom; inside a room it does nothing. **Esc**, in this order: closes an open sheet (also from the job dropdown); else leaves a room (a room hides the inquest panel); else closes the inquest panel (the agent card returns); else brings hidden panels back. A click on any building enters its room; a click on a robot opens its agent sheet; the sheet of a robot in the bay has an **INSPECTION** button that opens its inquest again.

**Camera** (build 4). Left drag turns the camera and the wheel zooms, as before. Holding the wheel down and dragging slides the camera over the ground, like dragging a map; the slide stops at the edge of the archipelago (the point the camera looks at stays inside the box of the islets and the Factory, never the open sea). The right button does nothing. Inside a room, a drag turns the room and the wheel zooms; there is no slide.

**Buttons and chips.** **START** (build 4) runs the selected agent's job; see "Jobs and START" below. REVOKE, RESTORE and KILL act on the selected agent's whole badge, each with a plain subtitle ("take the badge away now · the next call is refused", "give the badge back", "stop the running program"); since build 5 a REVOKE also ends a running job, the patrol included (JOB STOPPED, the robot stays in the pen), and RESTORE walks it out with the pill NO RUN, ready for START; RESET starts a fresh demo ("new chain, seed badges"; it also stops every launcher the gateway started and retires every Factory-made agent); SAVE REPLAY downloads every event seen. The **tool chips** on the agent card and on the agent sheet have five states: **green** (on the badge; a click pulls it, `POST /agents/{id}/tools/{tool}/revoke`), **paper with "+ grant"** (below the tier's ceiling but not on the badge; a click grants it, `.../restore`), **grey with a padlock** and a hint such as "Diamond only" (above the ceiling; a click still asks, and the gateway's 403 sentence shows as a toast while the chip shakes), **red, struck, "pulled"** (the operator took it off; a click gives it back), and **held** (the whole badge is revoked or killed; no clicks). **▲ PROMOTE / ▼ DEMOTE** next to the tier medal POST `/agents/{id}/tier`. In the office, **BUILD** POSTs `/agents` and **RETIRE** (Factory-made rows only) POSTs `/agents/{id}/retire`. Every button and chip reads "sent..." until the matching event arrives, or until the gateway answers that nothing changed. In the agent sheet, the buttons, chips and tier arrows act on that sheet's robot, even when another agent's run has moved the card meanwhile (build 4). In replay all of them are disabled, and START is not shown.

**The mode badge** (top right): `LIVE · model` or `LIVE · scripted` (the selected agent's `agent_mode`, so a judge sees whether a model or a fixed script drives the calls), `STALE` when the gateway has not answered for 3 s, `REPLAY` when a file plays (its second line reads `<date> · R or click: LIVE`, and a click on it goes back to live).

## The Factory island, the rooms, the inquest, the agent sheet

- **Minting.** On a 201 from BUILD the sheet closes, the iris shuts and the Factory floor opens: the production line builds the robot (press, head, the paint booth in the agent's colour, the tier medal stamped, one tool token per tool handed over) in about 8 s; then the iris opens on the island, the chimneys puff fast for 8 s, and the new robot appears at the hall door and walks to its yard square (the `created` event). Only the browser that clicked BUILD watches the floor; every other viewer sees the exterior. Build 4: the form also sends the job, and with "start working as soon as it is built" ticked (the default) this browser sends START once the line is done and the iris is back on the island (the floor gets 12 s at most), so the robot is seen leaving the yard for work.
- **Tiers.** `src/tiers.js` is the same table as `gateway/tiers.py` (change both or neither): Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, each a ceiling of tools and a plain line; the medal colours live here. The card reads "Gold badge · may speak for the company" and "n of 6 tools · ceiling m"; the robot wears a small medal on its chest plate. A `tier_changed` event moves the medal; a demotion trims the badge (the event's `badge=` says what is left) and the trimmed chips lock.
- **The bay.** A quarantine (the gateway pulled the badge itself) is drawn as QUARANTINED: the chief escorts the robot over the south bridge into the inspection bay and onto the lift. Since build 5 that includes the egress quarantine: a `send_email` from a badge that holds it, to an address outside the company, is stamped LEAK BLOCKED at the Mail gate, and the badge is pulled at once. A quarantined robot stays in the bay after its job stops, pill QUARANTINED. A killed run powers the robot down where it stands; the chief goes home and the Factory cart collects it and sets it down in the bay. An operator REVOKE still goes to the pen. RESTORE from the bay walks the robot back to the plaza.
- **The inquest panel.** On arrival in the bay a paper panel, `INSPECTION · <name>`, appears bottom-left, in the agent card's place (the card is hidden while the panel is open; Esc closes it and the card returns; the INSPECTION button on the robot's sheet reopens it), and types one line every 0.4 s with a ✓ or ✗: identity (client, tier), calls allowed and refused, each refused tool with its count and rule, the trigger (a quarantine by the gateway, or REVOKE or KILL by the operator, with its time), the timings, "rows hash-verified", then the verdict and the recommendation. It is `inquestOf(state, id)` in `src/reducer.js`, computed from this agent's rows of the log and nothing else; the gateway's `GET /agents/{id}/inquest` computes the same counts, trigger and timings from its chain (its verdict sentence can differ in wording: the island names every refused tool, the gateway the most-refused one; for an attempt to email outside the company both say the same, "Tried to send company data outside the company (send_email to <to>); the gateway refused it and pulled the badge at once.", with the recommendation "Do not restore until the task's inputs are cleaned: a document told it to leak."), and the panel's `raw report` link opens it. The panel is recomputed as new rows arrive, hides while a room is open, and stays while the robot stands in the bay unless Esc closes it.
- **The rooms** (`src/rooms.js`). Every building is a cutaway room in 3D that you rotate by dragging and zoom with the wheel; the HUD panels hide while a room is open, and the room's title bar (bottom-left) names it and holds `← island (Esc)`. The gatehouse: THE LEDGER chalkboard (the last 12 rows; click a row for its JSON, with one plain sentence above it), THE HASH CHAIN exhibit (the last 8 rows as receipts on two cords, each with `#seq`, the first six hex characters of its hash and `← #prev`, joined by chain links, sage when the link checks and brick when it does not), its caption, and the **TAMPER TEST** button: one character of a copy of a middle receipt's reason is changed, the copy is re-checked, that receipt and every one after it are marked broken for 3 s, then the real log is redrawn: "the real ledger is untouched · ✓ chain verified". The FILES tower, the GitHub studio, the Mail cottage and the Admin courthouse: one station per tool with a lamp that blinks green or red for 2.5 s on a live decision, and a board that says, per tool, what it does in plain words, who may do it here (every agent whose badge holds it, with its tier medal), the lowest tier that may ever hold it, and today's allowed and refused counts. The Factory office: the whiteboard of every agent (name, medal, tier, status, six tool squares, made by) and the office sheet. The Factory floor (the FLOOR plaque beside the door, or the big door itself): the line at rest, or building.
- **The agent sheet** (click a robot): the name and status pill, the medal and tier line with PROMOTE / DEMOTE, the role, where it is ("in the inspection bay", "in the Factory yard" and so on), the last action in words ("read a document · allowed · 86 ms", "tried to delete a file · refused · 9 ms"), calls allowed and refused, the note (blocked N ms after REVOKE, or after the tool was pulled, and how the run ended, since build 5 in words such as `run ended: job done · Clean up old temp files · 8 allowed, 0 refused`), identity (client prefix, made by the Factory or the seed), since build 4 its job (a picker, the SCRIPTED / AI switch and the job's line), START / REVOKE / RESTORE / KILL for this agent, the six chips, and the raw event JSON behind a closed `raw event (JSON)` line. Ledger rows still open their raw JSON, with a plain summary above it.

## Jobs and START (build 4)

The gateway's `GET /jobs` is `contract/jobs.json` plus the tools each job uses. `main.js` asks for it once at start, live only, and `normJobs` cleans the answer. Until it answers, and always in replay, the list is empty and every job control and START stays hidden, so the page still works on a gateway without build 4.

- **Where START is.** In the operator panel, above REVOKE: it acts on the selected agent, and its subtitle names the job and the brain ("run its job: Clean up old temp files · scripted") or says why it is greyed. And in each robot's sheet, where it acts on that robot.
- **What it sends.** `POST /agents/{id}/start` `{job, brain}` with the admin token. The job is the sheet's pick for that agent, else the job it was built with (`job` in `GET /agents`), else `suggestJob` for its badge. The brain is the sheet's SCRIPTED / AI switch, kept per agent: SCRIPTED at first, or AI for an agent whose last run was on the model. On a 202 the card and the operator panel follow that agent, its sheet closes so the walk, an escort or the inquest is not hidden, and the ticker reads `START Scout: Research Q3 and email the CEO (scripted), launcher pid N`. The button reads "sent..." until that agent's `run_started` clears it (the reducer's `start:<id>` ack); if none arrives in 25 s, a toast says the program did not report and points at the gateway console and `launcher/logs`. A refusal shows the gateway's sentence as `START refused: ...`.
- **`startState(a, ui)`** says whether START may be pressed, as `{ok, why}`, checking in this order: a recording ("a recording: nothing to start"); a START already sent ("starting its program…"); a RESET in progress ("RESET in progress…"); a run going ("working now · KILL stops it"); a badge row that is not ACTIVE ("badge KILLED: RESTORE first", or REVOKED); else "run its job now". `runActive(a)` answers "is a run going?" from the reducer's run flag, and from the plate only for a state without it.
- **The form** (Factory office). After the role come "job: what it does once it is built" (a dropdown), the job's line under it, the brain (SCRIPTED "same steps", AI "Nova 2 Lite", with "the gateway checks every call either way"), and beside BUILD the box "start working as soon as it is built", ticked. Until the operator picks a job, the dropdown follows the ticked tools, and so does the line, without redrawing the form (a redraw would eat the typing).
- **`suggestJob(jobs, tools)`**: of the ordinary jobs (not the patrol, never the attack drill), the one that uses the most of these tools and needs no other; else the patrol. Diamond with only `delete_file` gives cleanup; `read_file` and `search_files` give research; those two plus `send_email` give share.
- **`jobHint(job, tools)`** gives `{text, bad}`: "uses read_file, search_files, send_email · send_email not on this badge: the gateway refuses it" (bad, drawn red), or "… · all on this badge"; for the patrol, "uses every tool on its badge, one at a time". The form and the sheet show it after the job's own line: "deletes eight stale files in /drive/tmp · uses delete_file · all on this badge".
- **`normJobs(json)`** keeps `{job, name, line, tools, patrol}` for each row that has a job key and a name, drops tools outside the six, and turns anything that is not a list into `[]`.
- **The reducer's run flag.** Each agent now has `running`: `run_started` sets it and `run_stopped` clears it. The plate alone could not say whether a run is going: a RESTORE after a KILL used to paint the robot green with no run, which left START greyed out until RESET. Now RESTORE paints it green only while a run is going; otherwise the plate reads NO RUN and START can be pressed again. The roster also brings each agent's `job` and badge `status` (`withRoster`; a row without the `job` key keeps the one it had).
- **A run the gateway stopped for a RETIRE** (`run_stopped` with `stopped by the gateway: retire`) ends where the robot stands: the `retired` row comes next and its cart collects the robot there. Every other run that ended without a KILL still walks home and sinks into the hatch, unless its badge was pulled (build 5: a robot held in the pen or the bay stays there).
- **A job that finishes** (build 5). Every job but the patrol now runs its steps once and ends (the patrol loops until its badge is pulled), and the `run_stopped` row's reason reads `exit 0 (job done) · <the agent's last words>`: its `JOB DONE` line, its `JOB STOPPED` line, or for the AI brain the model's own reply. `stopWords(reason)` in `src/reducer.js` turns that into plain words for the card's note and the ticker: `run ended: job done · Clean up old temp files · 8 allowed, 0 refused`, `run ended: job done · said "..."` for a model's reply, `run ended: job stopped · badge revoked`, and for an AI run whose model could not answer (exit 4) `run ended: model unavailable · <the agent's MODEL UNAVAILABLE words>`; any other reason is shown as it is. A finished job shows the banner and the status pill JOB DONE, the robot walks home to the dock, and START can be pressed again. A job that stopped because the badge was pulled shows JOB STOPPED, and a robot held in the pen or the bay stays there, pill REVOKED or QUARANTINED, until RESTORE walks it out (pill NO RUN).
- **The card's choice.** A `run_started` no longer takes the card away from the agent the operator picked (KILL could otherwise hit the agent that just started). With no pick made, the card follows the newest run, and START (or BUILD with start ticked) selects its own agent.

`tests/jobs.test.mjs`: `normJobs` keeps the shape and drops junk; the Factory suggests the job that fits the ticked tools, never the drill; the job hint names a missing tool before anything runs; START is offered only for an ACTIVE badge with no run going (not in replay, not while sent); the roster carries the job and the badge status; the test's copy of the job list matches `contract/jobs.json` (keys, names, order); RESTORE after a KILL leaves NO RUN and START can be pressed, while RESTORE during a run stays green; a run the gateway stopped for a RETIRE ends where it stands; retiring a robot held in the pen opens the rope first; and, since build 5, `stopWords` turns each kind of `run_stopped` reason into plain words, a finished job shows JOB DONE (banner and pill, the summary on the card, the walk home, START again), and an egress deny is stamped LEAK BLOCKED at the Mail gate, quarantined at once, held in the bay after its job stops, with the egress verdict. `tests/reducer.test.mjs` also checks that START's "sent..." clears on that agent's `run_started` and on nothing else.

## Build 5: LEAK BLOCKED, finished jobs, the card

- **LEAK BLOCKED.** A `deny` with rule `egress` whose reason says "refused: outside the company" (`isLeak` in `src/reducer.js`, which reads the text after the reason's last " refused: ", so a `to` that spells those words cannot fake it) is drawn at the Mail gate like an allow-list refusal, but the stamp reads LEAK BLOCKED and the banner `LEAK BLOCKED <address>` (the `to=` of the row's `args_summary`). The gateway's quarantine row follows at once (`quarantine: 1 refused call (egress)`), so the chief escorts the robot to the inspection bay, where it stays, and the inquest opens with the egress verdict. An `egress` refusal of a malformed address is an ordinary DENIED stamp at the same gate.
- **Finished jobs**: JOB DONE or JOB STOPPED, the walk home, or held in the pen or the bay (see "A job that finishes" above).
- **The card.** The agent tabs wrap onto more rows, so any number of agents fits (the card grows upward). The line under the name reads `client <prefix> · scripted` or `· AI` (the agent's latest `agent_mode`), then a job's name: the run's job while a run is going, else the one START would run (the sheet's pick, else the job it was built with, `job` in `GET /agents`, else `suggestJob`'s), on one line (the whole text on hover). An allowed `tools/list` no longer replaces the `last:` line, which keeps the last tool call; a refused `tools/list` (after a quarantine, say) still shows.

Nothing here changes what the island may do: it still draws only rows of the log, and its buttons still only POST.

## How the island works (ten lines)

1. `src/reducer.js` has one pure function, `apply(state, event)`: it returns the new state and a list of effects. It applies each `seq` once, in order. Nothing else changes state. It also holds `inquestOf`, the report computed from the log. Since build 4 each agent also carries `running` (set by `run_started`, cleared by `run_stopped`), its `job` and its badge `status`.
2. `src/main.js` feeds it: live, it polls `GET /events?since_seq=<cursor>` (and reads `GET /agents` for the roster, `GET /tiers` for the tier table and, since build 4, `GET /jobs` for the job book); in replay, it plays a file at the recording's own pace. Same `apply` for both. It also wires the buttons (START among them since build 4), the chips, the tier arrows, the form, the keys and the clicks on robots and buildings.
3. Each event becomes a **script** for a robot (walk to a gate, show the ID card, the gate opens or refuses, a guard escorts it back; build 3 adds the `build`, `cart` and `recycle` steps) plus instant effects (plate colour, banner, stamp, slip, packet, lamp, padlock, medal). `src/actors.js` plays the scripts: the robots in six colours, the five guards and the Factory cart, walking on the waypoint graph of `src/layout.js`.
4. `src/world.js` builds the islands, bridges, gates, booths, the pen, the bin and the scenery; `src/buildings.js` the five buildings; `src/factory.js` the Factory island (the arch, the hall and its chimneys, the yard, the bay, the cart's home); `src/kit.js` is Mytopia's recipes gathered into one file; `src/effects.js` draws slips, packets, lamps, smoke and the chimneys' puffs; `src/scene.js` puts them together and gives `main.js` one small API, which since build 4 includes the camera (the slide and its box, and `recenter` for C).
5. `src/hud.js` is every word you read outside the rooms: the agent card with its medal and chips, the operator panel with its subtitles, the banner, the mode badge, the ticker, the agent sheet with its job picker and START, the office sheet with the job fields, the inquest panel, the name tags, the DENIED stamps and the ID cards anchored over the gate that acted, with one collision pass so nothing overlaps. Its plain-word helpers touch no DOM, so the tests read them; build 4 added `startState`, `runActive`, `normJobs`, `suggestJob` and `jobHint`, and build 5 taught `statusPill` to say JOB DONE and JOB STOPPED.
6. The **ID card** a robot raises shows its real `client` id, the scope, and the token line from the last event that mentioned them (`token OK · expires in 300 s`, or `signature OK · expires in 243 s` from a refusal); with no such event it says `token: checked by the gateway`, never a made-up number. A `created` row names the new client's prefix.
7. A **per-tool pull** strikes that chip red "pulled", the badge count drops, a padlock hangs on that islet's gate and booth, and the next call of that tool is refused at its gate with the escort; the card's note says how many ms after the pull it was blocked. A **grant** turns a paper chip green, and the next call at that gate is allowed. A **promotion** opens padlocks; a **demotion** trims the badge to the new ceiling and locks the trimmed chips.
8. A **quarantine** (the gateway pulled the badge itself after repeated refusals, or since build 5 at once after an email to an address outside the company, stamped LEAK BLOCKED at the Mail gate) sends the robot to the inspection bay with the chief, under the banner and pill QUARANTINED, and opens the inquest; a **kill** powers the robot down and the cart brings it to the bay; the REVOKE button never waits for a quarantine. `created` builds a robot; `retired` wheels it into the Factory and drops it from the roster.
9. `src/rooms.js` holds the seven interiors and paints their boards from the log and the roster; `src/ledger.js` keeps `verifyChain`, which recomputes every row's SHA-256 in this browser (`✓ chain verified`, or where it broke); `src/room.js` is a four-line shim that re-exports `rooms.js` and `ledger.js` so build-2 imports still resolve; it holds no room of its own. `src/arch.js` holds the architecture drawing; `src/tiers.js` the tier table.
10. The buttons only POST; in REPLAY they are disabled and START is hidden; if the gateway stops, the picture freezes and the badge says STALE. Nothing is ever invented on the client.
