# island/: the face of MYTOPIA

The island draws the gateway's decision log. It never decides anything. Every animation is one row of `GET /events`, and the only things it sends back are clicks: the operator buttons, the six tool chips, PROMOTE and DEMOTE, the NEW AGENT form and RETIRE, each a POST that changes nothing on screen until the gateway's own event comes back. One exception, by design: when the gateway refuses a grant above a badge's tier with HTTP 403, its sentence shows as a banner; nothing was written, so there is no row.

## Answers to the brief's three questions

1. **Stack.** Plain ES modules plus **three.js 0.184** (WebGL), **no build step**. The renderer is Mytopia's `<three-d-stage>` web component: an orthographic camera, rendered at half resolution, with an ink-outline post-process for the pixel look. Every word on the island is crisp DOM laid over the canvas; inside the rooms the text is painted on 2048-pixel canvas textures, and the stage renders at full resolution while a room is open (`setPixel`, the one method added to the component this weekend). The gateway serves the `island/` folder as is; three.js and the fonts are vendored, so there are no CDN or network calls.
2. **Buildings.** An archipelago (`docs/ISLAND_V2.md`): each tool lives on its own islet, reached over one bridge with a gate and a guard. **Files** = the FILES tower, **GitHub** = the glass studio, **Mail** = the cottage, **Admin** = the courthouse, and the **gatehouse** on the main island is the gateway itself (its interior is the ledger room). Since build 3 (`docs/BUILD3.md`) there is also the **Factory island** to the south, over a plank bridge with no gate (it is the operator's own ground): the brick hall with its sawtooth roof and two chimneys (the office and the production floor are inside), the yard where built robots wait, the open inspection bay with its lift, scanner and screen, and the cart.
3. **Pre-existing vs new.** Pre-existing (from Mytopia, before this weekend): `three-d-stage.js` (one method added on Saturday night, `setPixel`) and the recipes gathered into `src/kit.js` (palette, materials, primitives), the terrain and scenery recipes, the building shapes and the small robot. Everything else is new this weekend. The full list is in [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md).

## Run it

Served by the gateway at `http://127.0.0.1:8787/` (it serves its own `/config.js`). Replays: `?replay=/replay/demo3.json` (the Factory run on real AWS, Saturday 7:55 PM: 56 rows, real hashes, so the ledger reads chain verified), `?replay=/replay/demo3-local.json` (the same flow in local mode), `?replay=/replay/demo.json` (the recorded 4:18 PM build-2 run), `?replay=fixture.json` (the contract's 21 events), `?replay=tests/additions.json` (hand-written: a per-tool revoke and a quarantine), `?replay=tests/factory.json` (hand-written: the Factory story, with placeholder hashes). Tests: `node --test island/tests/*.test.mjs` (66).

**Keys.** **F** (or the FACTORY button, or a click on the Factory hall) opens the Factory office sheet: the roster and the NEW AGENT form. **G** (or a click on the gatehouse) opens the ledger room. **H** hides every panel; the pill bottom-left, or H again, brings them back. **?** opens the help sheet: six illustrated lines (the robot, a gate, the guard, the pen, the Factory, the ledger) and the keys. **T** shows every event as a table. **A** opens the architecture sheet. **R** replays the fixture. **Esc**, in this order: closes an open sheet; else closes the inquest panel (the agent card returns); else brings hidden panels back; else leaves a room. A click on any building enters its room; a click on a robot opens its agent sheet; the sheet of a robot in the bay has an **INSPECTION** button that opens its inquest again.

**Mouse.** REVOKE, RESTORE and KILL act on the selected agent's whole badge, each with a plain subtitle ("take the badge away now · the next call is refused", "give the badge back", "stop the running program"); RESET starts a fresh demo ("new chain, seed badges"; it also retires every Factory-made agent); SAVE REPLAY downloads every event seen. The **tool chips** on the agent card and on the agent sheet have five states: **green** (on the badge; a click pulls it, `POST /agents/{id}/tools/{tool}/revoke`), **paper with "+ grant"** (below the tier's ceiling but not on the badge; a click grants it, `.../restore`), **grey with a padlock** and a hint such as "Diamond only" (above the ceiling; a click still asks, and the gateway's 403 sentence shows as a banner while the chip shakes), **red, struck, "pulled"** (the operator took it off; a click gives it back), and **held** (the whole badge is revoked or killed; no clicks). **▲ PROMOTE / ▼ DEMOTE** next to the tier medal POST `/agents/{id}/tier`. In the office, **BUILD** POSTs `/agents` and **RETIRE** (Factory-made rows only) POSTs `/agents/{id}/retire`. Every button and chip reads "sent..." until the matching event arrives. In replay all of them are disabled.

**The mode badge** (top right): `LIVE · model` or `LIVE · scripted` (the selected agent's `agent_mode`, so a judge sees whether a model or a fixed script drives the calls), `STALE` when the gateway has not answered for 3 s, `REPLAY` when a file plays.

## The Factory island, the rooms, the inquest, the agent sheet

- **Minting.** On a 201 from BUILD the sheet closes, the iris shuts and the Factory floor opens: the production line builds the robot (press, head, the paint booth in the agent's colour, the tier medal stamped, one tool token per tool handed over) in about 8 s; then the iris opens on the island, the chimneys puff fast for 8 s, and the new robot appears at the hall door and walks to its yard square (the `created` event). Only the browser that clicked BUILD watches the floor; every other viewer sees the exterior.
- **Tiers.** `src/tiers.js` is the same table as `gateway/tiers.py` (change both or neither): Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, each a ceiling of tools and a plain line; the medal colours live here. The card reads "Gold badge · may speak for the company" and "n of 6 tools · ceiling m"; the robot wears a small medal on its chest plate. A `tier_changed` event moves the medal; a demotion trims the badge (the event's `badge=` says what is left) and the trimmed chips lock.
- **The bay.** A quarantine (the gateway pulled the badge itself) is drawn as QUARANTINED: the chief escorts the robot over the south bridge into the inspection bay and onto the lift. A killed run powers the robot down where it stands; the chief goes home and the Factory cart collects it and sets it down in the bay. An operator REVOKE still goes to the pen. RESTORE from the bay walks the robot back to the plaza.
- **The inquest panel.** On arrival in the bay a paper panel, `INSPECTION · <name>`, appears bottom-left, in the agent card's place (the card is hidden while the panel is open; Esc closes it and the card returns; the INSPECTION button on the robot's sheet reopens it), and types one line every 0.4 s with a ✓ or ✗: identity (client, tier), calls allowed and refused, each refused tool with its count and rule, the trigger (a quarantine by the gateway, or REVOKE or KILL by the operator, with its time), the timings, "rows hash-verified", then the verdict and the recommendation. It is `inquestOf(state, id)` in `src/reducer.js`, computed from this agent's rows of the log and nothing else; the gateway's `GET /agents/{id}/inquest` computes the same counts, trigger and timings from its chain (its verdict sentence can differ in wording: the island names every refused tool, the gateway the most-refused one), and the panel's `raw report` link opens it. The panel is recomputed as new rows arrive, hides while a room is open, and stays while the robot stands in the bay unless Esc closes it.
- **The rooms** (`src/rooms.js`). Every building is a cutaway room in 3D that you rotate by dragging and zoom with the wheel; the HUD panels hide while a room is open, and the room's title bar (bottom-left) names it and holds `← island (Esc)`. The gatehouse: THE LEDGER chalkboard (the last 12 rows; click a row for its JSON, with one plain sentence above it), THE HASH CHAIN exhibit (the last 8 rows as receipts on two cords, each with `#seq`, the first six hex characters of its hash and `← #prev`, joined by chain links, sage when the link checks and brick when it does not), its caption, and the **TAMPER TEST** button: one character of a copy of a middle receipt's reason is changed, the copy is re-checked, that receipt and every one after it are marked broken for 3 s, then the real log is redrawn: "the real ledger is untouched · ✓ chain verified". The FILES tower, the GitHub studio, the Mail cottage and the Admin courthouse: one station per tool with a lamp that blinks green or red for 2.5 s on a live decision, and a board that says, per tool, what it does in plain words, who may do it here (every agent whose badge holds it, with its tier medal), the lowest tier that may ever hold it, and today's allowed and refused counts. The Factory office: the whiteboard of every agent (name, medal, tier, status, six tool squares, made by) and the office sheet. The Factory floor (the FLOOR plaque beside the door, or the big door itself): the line at rest, or building.
- **The agent sheet** (click a robot): the name and status pill, the medal and tier line with PROMOTE / DEMOTE, the role, where it is ("in the inspection bay", "in the Factory yard" and so on), the last action in words ("read a document · allowed · 86 ms", "tried to delete a file · refused · 9 ms"), calls allowed and refused, the note (blocked N ms after REVOKE, or after the tool was pulled), identity (client prefix, made by the Factory or the seed), the six chips, REVOKE / RESTORE / KILL for this agent, and the raw event JSON behind a closed `raw event (JSON)` line. Ledger rows still open their raw JSON, with a plain summary above it.

## How the island works (ten lines)

1. `src/reducer.js` has one pure function, `apply(state, event)`: it returns the new state and a list of effects. It applies each `seq` once, in order. Nothing else changes state. It also holds `inquestOf`, the report computed from the log.
2. `src/main.js` feeds it: live, it polls `GET /events?since_seq=<cursor>` (and reads `GET /agents` for the roster and `GET /tiers` for the tier table); in replay, it plays a file at the recording's own pace. Same `apply` for both. It also wires the buttons, the chips, the tier arrows, the form, the keys and the clicks on robots and buildings.
3. Each event becomes a **script** for a robot (walk to a gate, show the ID card, the gate opens or refuses, a guard escorts it back; build 3 adds the `build`, `cart` and `recycle` steps) plus instant effects (plate colour, banner, stamp, slip, packet, lamp, padlock, medal). `src/actors.js` plays the scripts: the robots in six colours, the five guards and the Factory cart, walking on the waypoint graph of `src/layout.js`.
4. `src/world.js` builds the islands, bridges, gates, booths, the pen, the bin and the scenery; `src/buildings.js` the five buildings; `src/factory.js` the Factory island (the arch, the hall and its chimneys, the yard, the bay, the cart's home); `src/kit.js` is Mytopia's recipes gathered into one file; `src/effects.js` draws slips, packets, lamps, smoke and the chimneys' puffs; `src/scene.js` puts them together and gives `main.js` one small API.
5. `src/hud.js` is every word you read outside the rooms: the agent card with its medal and chips, the operator panel with its subtitles, the banner, the mode badge, the ticker, the agent sheet, the office sheet, the inquest panel, the name tags, the DENIED stamps and the ID cards anchored over the gate that acted, with one collision pass so nothing overlaps.
6. The **ID card** a robot raises shows its real `client` id, the scope, and the token line from the last event that mentioned them (`token OK · expires in 300 s`, or `signature OK · expires in 243 s` from a refusal); with no such event it says `token: checked by the gateway`, never a made-up number. A `created` row names the new client's prefix.
7. A **per-tool pull** strikes that chip red "pulled", the badge count drops, a padlock hangs on that islet's gate and booth, and the next call of that tool is refused at its gate with the escort; the card's note says how many ms after the pull it was blocked. A **grant** turns a paper chip green, and the next call at that gate is allowed. A **promotion** opens padlocks; a **demotion** trims the badge to the new ceiling and locks the trimmed chips.
8. A **quarantine** (the gateway pulled the badge itself after repeated refusals) sends the robot to the inspection bay with the chief, under the banner and pill QUARANTINED, and opens the inquest; a **kill** powers the robot down and the cart brings it to the bay; the REVOKE button never waits for a quarantine. `created` builds a robot; `retired` wheels it into the Factory and drops it from the roster.
9. `src/rooms.js` holds the seven interiors and paints their boards from the log and the roster; `src/ledger.js` keeps `verifyChain`, which recomputes every row's SHA-256 in this browser (`✓ chain verified`, or where it broke); `src/room.js` is a four-line shim that re-exports `rooms.js` and `ledger.js` so build-2 imports still resolve; it holds no room of its own. `src/arch.js` holds the architecture drawing; `src/tiers.js` the tier table.
10. The buttons only POST; in REPLAY they are disabled; if the gateway stops, the picture freezes and the badge says STALE. Nothing is ever invented on the client.
